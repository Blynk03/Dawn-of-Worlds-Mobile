Privacy policy

Dawn of Worlds · Effective November 12, 2026

Dawn of Worlds is a small, non-commercial app for playing the world-building game Dawn of Worlds with friends. It is made by Blynk03 (contact: blynk03@gmail.com). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

Data Why
Your email address To sign you in (we email you a sign-in link or code) and to keep your account.
The name you play under and your gods (names, spheres, colours, sigils) Shown to the other players in your worlds.
What you create in the game: worlds, actions and their descriptions, races, cities and the like, votes, comments and reactions This is the game itself, shared with the players in that world.
A push notification token for your device, if you allow notifications To tell you when it's your turn, when a round begins, and similar.
Basic technical logs kept by our hosting provider (such as IP addresses and request times) To run the service and keep it secure.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or payment details. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Who can see it

What you do in a world, and the name and gods you play with, are visible to the other players in that world. Your email address is not shown to other players.

Where it's kept, and who helps us

We don't sell or share your data. A few services process it on our behalf, only to run the app:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in service. It stores your data, encrypted at rest, and sends sign-in emails.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: deliver push notifications to your device.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the web version of the app and this page.

All data travels between the app and these services encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. Records of notifications sent are kept for troubleshooting and are deleted with your account.

Deleting your account

In the app, open My God → Delete my account…. You can also do this in the web version, or email blynk03@gmail.com from your account's email address and we'll delete it within 30 days. See Delete your account for exactly what is deleted.

Deleting your account removes your sign-in and email address, comments, reactions, notification devices and settings. In worlds that have already started, your gods' past actions stay in that world's shared history (with your name replaced by “Departed player”) so the other players' game still makes sense; your god takes no more turns.

Children

Dawn of Worlds is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
