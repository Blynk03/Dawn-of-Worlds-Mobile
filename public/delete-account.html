Delete your Dawn of Worlds account

Dawn of Worlds, by Blynk03

How to delete it

  1. In the app (Android, or the web version on any device): sign in, open My God, tap Delete my account…, then Delete my account. It happens straight away.
  2. By email, if you can't use the app: write to blynk03@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, asking us to delete your account. We'll do it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and email address
  • Your comments and reactions
  • Your devices for notifications, notification settings and nudges, and records of notifications sent to you
  • Worlds that haven't started yet: you leave them, and any that only you are in are deleted

What is kept

In worlds that have already started, the game is shared with your friends, so your gods' past actions stay in that world's history, with your name replaced by “Departed player”. Your god takes no more turns, and if you hosted the world, the next player becomes host. Nothing kept is linked to your email address.
